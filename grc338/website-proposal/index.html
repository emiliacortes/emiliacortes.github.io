Final project proposal

Introduction

Future is Vintage

Future is Vintage will be an e-commerce website dedicated to reselling vintage handbags from Coach. The website centers on the idea that vintage design never goes out of style and can be long lasting. The website will feature different collections, condition transparency, and storytelling that highlights the craftsmanship and heritage of each piece. The visual identity will blend nostalgic elements with clean, modern design through the use of a light color palette and high quality images.

Target audience

The intended audience leans more towards fashion-conscious young adults, especially those ages 18-35 who are drawn to 1990s and early 2000s trends. This also includes sustainability-minded consumers who value secondhand fashion. These buyers are motivated by environmental awareness and prefer investing in durable, well-crafted pieces rather than fast fashion. Additionally, collectors and leather enthusiasts may seek out older Coach styles for their craftsmanship and nostalgic appeal.

Home

[Collection of different coach bags available to purchase.]

At Future is Vintage, we take pride in our ability to give vintage bags a new life. We receive bags in need of some cleaning and love, and fix them so they have a second chance to be worn. Due to Coach's high standard for quality, their bags are made to last. Vintage doesn't necessarily mean old since we make these bags brand new again.

About

[2nd photo of collection of different coach bags available to purchase.]

We believe the future of fashion is circular. Where iconic pieces are rediscovered, restyled, and reimagined. By giving vintage Coach bags a second life, we offer sustainable luxury that feels both nostalgic and modern. Every bag is carefully sourced, authenticated, and selected for its character and longevity.
